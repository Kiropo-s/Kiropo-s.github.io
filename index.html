A little about me

I'm pursuing a master's degree in Computer Technology at East China Normal University. Before that, I studied Computer Science and Technology at Hefei University of Technology.

My interests sit at the intersection of computer vision and graphics: reconstructing 3D scenes, exploring new views, and making rendering more efficient. I enjoy working through both the algorithms and the systems that bring them to the screen. My background includes programming contests and building a renderer from scratch in C++.

What I'm working on

Research · In progress · 2026–present

Making large 3D scenes lighter to render

My current research explores view-adaptive Gaussian skipping for large-scale 3D Gaussian Splatting, aiming to reduce rendering work while preserving visual quality and the underlying scene representation.

First-author work, in preparation.

Personal project · Undergraduate thesis · 2026

MyRenderer

A C++ renderer I built with both rasterization and ray tracing. It supports switching between the two pipelines at runtime, with an interactive panel for exploring rendering parameters.

Features include PBR, shadow mapping, normal mapping, and compute-shader ray tracing with BVH acceleration.
